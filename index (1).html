The problem we solve

The gap is institutional, not individual

Organisations with non-native English speaking populations — in their classrooms, workplaces, or programs — consistently find that designing effective language and integration support is harder than it looks. It sits at the intersection of language education, program design, and institutional change: a combination of expertise that rarely exists under one roof.


The result is that capable, motivated people are held back not by lack of ability, but by systems that were not designed with them in mind. That is a fixable problem. And fixing it is what Pacha Learning Systems does.

What we do

We are architects, not teachers

We design the system, source the people to run it, and make sure it works. We are never standing in the classroom. Every engagement ends with an organisation that is more capable of serving its non-native English speaking population than it was before we arrived — and that capability stays after we leave.

01
Needs Assessment & Discovery
A standalone diagnostic engagement. We assess where language and cultural barriers are creating the most risk or lost opportunity, and deliver a written report with prioritised, actionable recommendations.
02
Program Design & Launch
Our core 90-day engagement. We design a complete language and workforce integration program tailored to your population, sector, and goals — and deliver a blueprint your organisation can act on.
03
Faculty & Staff Development
A training program for the people inside your organisation who work directly with non-native English speaking populations. We build internal capacity so your organisation can serve this population without ongoing external support.
04
Institutional Audit & Redesign
A comprehensive review of your entire service model for non-native English speaking populations — intake, advising, onboarding, communications. We map the friction and redesign for inclusion.
05
Strategic Retainer
Ongoing monthly support as a fractional strategic partner during implementation. One weekly call, async availability, one on-site visit per month, and one written deliverable. You keep the momentum; we keep the standard.
06
Fractional Program Director
For organisations that need a head of language and workforce integration but can't justify a full-time hire. We own outcomes, manage educators, and report to senior leadership — typically two to three days per week for six to twelve months.
